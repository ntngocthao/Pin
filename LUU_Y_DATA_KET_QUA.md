# Luu y ve thu muc data/ va results/ (data & results folders)

Ban zip nay **khong bao gom**:
- `data/` (~94 MB): du lieu CSV thu goc cua 4 bo du lieu XJTU, HUST, MIT, TJU.
- `results/` (~67 MB): output tho (.npy) tu qua trinh chay `main_*.py` nhieu lan.

Ly do: hai thu muc nay rat nang va khong can thiet de kiem tra code da chuyen doi
sang Python/thu vien moi hoat dong dung. Neu ban can chay lai toan bo qua trinh
huan luyen, hay tai lai hai thu muc nay tu repo goc:

https://github.com/wang-fujin/PINN4SOH

roi dat vao dung vi tri `data/` va `results/` ngang hang voi cac file `main_*.py`.

Thu muc `pretrained model/` (696 KB, model da huan luyen san) va
`results analysis/processed results*` (~600 KB, ket qua da xu ly de ve bieu do)
van duoc giu nguyen vi kich thuoc nho va can thiet de chay thu cac script
trong `plotter/`.
